
Privacy Policy for Hepatos

Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Hepatos ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information when you use the Hepatos mobile application (the "App").

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information or usage data from users of the Hepatos app.
• No Account Required: You can use the App without creating an account or providing your name, email address, or contact details.
• Local Storage Only: Any preferences, settings, or entries you make within the App are stored strictly locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
• No Third-Party Tracking: Hepatos does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store any of your personal data on external servers, we do not retain any user records. Any data stored locally on your device can be permanently removed at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the App from your device.

3. Children’s Privacy

Since Hepatos does not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if we add new features to the App. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
